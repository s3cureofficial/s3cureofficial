### Hi, this is S³cure 👋

Tools that keep your servers and communities safe – simple enough for people who don't write code.

#### What we build

🛡️ **S³cure** – your own Discord bot that stops scams and raids. Your name and avatar, no code and no hosting of your own. You turn on what it should do, per server.

🖥️ **[S3cure SSH Client](https://github.com/s3cureofficial/s3cure-ssh-client)** – SSH & SFTP client for macOS, Windows and Linux: terminal, files with drag & drop, editor and tunnels in one window. Free and open source.
[Download the latest version →](https://github.com/s3cureofficial/s3cure-ssh-client/releases/latest)
